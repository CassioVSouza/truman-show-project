# Code of Conduct

We want a respectful, welcoming space for everyone who works on or discusses this project.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good faith. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people writing in a second language (English and Portuguese are both welcome in discussions).
- Keep discussion on topic.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, doxxing, or sharing others' private information.
- Posting secrets or credentials, yours or anyone's.
- Trying to interfere with the live Resident or the infrastructure outside the documented contribution process.

## Note on the Resident's content

The Resident is intentionally unfiltered (see docs/01, principle 16). What it reads or writes is the experiment, not a conduct violation, and contributors are not expected to defend or approve of it. This code governs how *people* treat each other.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, close threads, or block users who break these rules. Report problems privately through GitHub (a private security advisory or a direct message to the maintainer).
